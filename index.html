03  /  The person behind the resume

The streak never broke.

I have been starting things since high school. At 17 I ran K Productions, a web design and advertising agency that reached $100K ARR before I graduated. My summers went to Dignity Health, working accounting, resources, the ICU and the ER. I translated for Vietnamese patients at a community clinic, taught at my church, and edited video for anyone who asked.

College at ASU brought Accompanian, which was acquired. Then Solisa, venture-backed and scaled to millions in revenue with a 12-person team and enterprise clients I closed myself. DoorStopper came and went inside a single quarter, which taught me more about pivots than the two years before it.

Now Kijestic, Dermetry and Dasom, where I am testing a bigger idea: that one person with the right AI infrastructure can operate like a company. I am fluent in English and Vietnamese, based in Arizona, and I answer my own email.
